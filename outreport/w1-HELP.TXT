flash - put a boot image back into the phone with no adb
=====================================================

  1. copy the image onto this volume as BOOTIMG.BIN
     (8.3, uppercase; about 18 MB for this project)
  2. flush the volume so Windows writes the directory entry
     (do not eject: an eject unloads the medium on this host,
     and the LUN stays gone until the cable is re-plugged)
  3. write QEAF.CMD containing exactly one line:

       flash recovery BOOTIMG.BIN confirm

  4. flush again, wait ten seconds, then read RESPONSE.TXT
     with build/read-report-raw.ps1 to see what happened.

Checked before a single byte is written:
  - the target is 'recovery' and nothing else, and the word
    confirm has to be on the line: the boot slot that runs
    Android is never writable from here
  - /dev/disk/by-name/recovery resolves, and to a node under
    /dev, so a stale symlink cannot point somewhere surprising
  - the payload starts with the ANDROID! boot header magic
  - the payload fits the partition, sized from the kernel's own
    /proc/partitions rather than from the image's word for it
Afterwards the partition is read back and compared with these
clusters, and the result is printed. A flash that cannot be
verified says so; it does not quietly succeed.

Writing recovery while booted from recovery is safe here: the
kernel and this initramfs are already in RAM, which is the whole
reason p77 is the slot that runs qeafinit.
Volume: 32 MiB FAT16, 4 KiB clusters; the four owned files
hold clusters 2..42 and everything from 43 on is free for
the host (8135 clusters, 31 MiB).
